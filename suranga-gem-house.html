Our Story

From Sri Lanka's
Earth to Your Hands

Nestled in Ratnapura — the "City of Gems" — Suranga Gem House has served collectors, jewellers and investors worldwide for over two decades. We source directly from trusted local miners, ensuring every stone tells an authentic story.

Our expert team personally inspects and selects each gem for colour, clarity, cut and carat weight. Whether you need a single investment stone or a bulk wholesale order, we are your trusted partner in Ceylon gems.
